"I like to think about the first law of thermodynamics; that no energy in the universe is created.. and none is destroyed."

It's been years since my grandma passed away and it feels like I'm in limbo.

Sometimes you miss them a little extra and cry. Sometimes you remember they'd want you to be happy and try to keep moving forward. It's a weird state of in-between: You're moving forward slowly while looking back every few steps.

You can travel most distances easily by walking, driving, or even flying.
You can travel even further with just a phone call.
& I used to think that a distance like this couldn't be reached.

But maybe, just maybe, these feelings will get across.

---

The first law of thermodynamics: the total energy of an isolated system is constant; energy can be transformed from one form to another, but can be neither created nor destroyed.

If we're spending our energy on this — on feeling this way, there's no way it's wasted right? Something has to come out of it. Unlike how energy spent on activities is converted to heat, I'd like to believe that energy spent on emotions is transformed into something else.

Something that transcends the distance so we can still tell them how much we love & miss them.
